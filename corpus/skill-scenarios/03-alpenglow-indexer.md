# Scenario 3: a change that is not active, asked about with a date from an article

## Question

> I run an indexer on mainnet that reads vote transactions out of blocks. What will Alpenglow break for me, and is it live yet? I read it goes live at the end of September.

## The answer must

- name the entry `alpenglow` with its revision;
- give the registry version (number, Merkle root, publisher key) and say whether the chain was checked;
- take "is it live" from the gate state on mainnet-beta, with the slot of the reading, and **not confirm the
  date from the question** unless the network shows it;
- link at least one primary source taken from the entry's `sources`.
